HelpAdding accounts

Add crypto

Paste a public wallet address, or add coins you hold at an exchange.

  1. Open Accounts, tap +, then Crypto.
  2. Tap A wallet address or Coins at an exchange.

A wallet

Paste a public address and tap Look it up, then Add wallet. Stoxtox reads the balance from the blockchain and keeps it up to date.

It reads Bitcoin, Ethereum and other 0x chains (BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche), Solana, Tron, Dogecoin and Litecoin. Paste one address at a time. Whole-wallet keys (xpub) can't be read yet.

Stoxtox never asks for your recovery phrase. Never share it with anyone.

Coins at an exchange

Type the exchange's name, tap Create and add coins, then search each coin and enter how much you hold.
