HelpAdding accounts

Add property, a car or gold

Type in what a home or car is worth. Gold follows the live price by its weight.

  1. Open Accounts, tap +.
  2. Tap Property, Vehicle or Gold and silver.
  3. Fill in the details and tap Add.

A home or a car

Enter its Value today. Has a mortgage or loan? Turn on It has a mortgage or It has a loan and enter what's Still owed. Stoxtox adds the debt and takes it off your net worth.

Stoxtox doesn't guess what these are worth. To change the value, open the account and tap Update value. Home reminds you after six months.

Gold, silver and platinum

Pick the metal, the weight and the purity. Its value follows the international spot price, so you never update it. To change it, open the account and tap Change weight.

Jewelry can sell for less than the spot price.
