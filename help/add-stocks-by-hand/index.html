Add stocks by hand

Make an account and add each stock, ETF or fund with how many shares you own.

  1. Open the Accounts tab and tap +.
  2. Tap Stocks, ETFs and funds, then Add stocks one by one.
  3. Type your broker's name, pick an account type and tap Create and add stocks.
  4. Search for a stock, enter How many shares and, if you like, What you paid.
  5. Tap the Add button. Add the next one, or close when you're done.

Leave what you paid blank and Stoxtox uses today's price. Add your own to see your return.

Have a lot of holdings? Import a file or screenshots instead and add them all at once.
