Why can't I connect my broker?

Some institutions haven't approved Stoxtox yet. You can still bring their accounts in.

Each broker has to approve Stoxtox before it can connect through Plaid. Some haven't yet. Their chip in Stoxtox says Import instead of Connect.

When one is approved, it starts to connect without an app update.

Until then

  • Import a file or screenshots from that broker. It takes a minute.
  • Or add the stocks by hand.

Later, you can connect the account and remove the imported copy, so it isn't counted twice.

Connecting is only for US accounts for now.
