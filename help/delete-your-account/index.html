Delete your account

Deleting removes your sign-in and portfolio from our servers and this iPhone.

  1. Open the You tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account again to confirm.

Your sign-in, portfolio and watchlist are deleted from our servers and from this iPhone. Connected banks and brokers are disconnected.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Plus. Cancel it in Settings too, or Apple keeps billing it.

If you signed in with Apple, you confirm with Apple once more.
