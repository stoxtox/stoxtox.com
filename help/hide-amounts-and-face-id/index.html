Hide amounts and lock with Face ID

Hide every figure with one switch, and lock Stoxtox behind Face ID.

Hide amounts

  1. Open the You tab.
  2. Turn on Hide amounts.

Your own amounts are hidden across the app, so you can open it in public.

Face ID lock

  1. Open the You tab.
  2. Turn on Face ID lock and confirm with Face ID.

Stoxtox then asks for Face ID each time it opens, and hides itself in the app switcher. You need a passcode set on your iPhone to use it.
