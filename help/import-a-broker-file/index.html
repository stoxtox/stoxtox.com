Import holdings from your broker

Bring in every holding at once from a CSV, a PDF statement or screenshots.

  1. Download your positions or holdings from your broker, as a CSV or PDF. Screenshots of your holdings work too.
  2. In Stoxtox, open Accounts, tap +, then Stocks, ETFs and funds.
  3. Tap Choose a file or Choose screenshots (up to 8).
  4. Check what was found, pick your broker and account type, and tap Add.

Your file is read on your iPhone. Only what you confirm is added. You can also share a file into Stoxtox from another app.

If something's left out

The review lists anything it skipped with a reason, like No price or Option, not a share. Holdings with no price yet are kept by their shares, and counted once they're priced.

Common messages

  • "That's a history of trades." Export your current positions instead.
  • "We couldn't find a symbol and a quantity column." Use a file with a ticker and a shares column.
  • "This PDF has a password." Enter the statement's password when asked.

Updating later

Import a newer file for the same account and choose Update. Turn on Remove, they were sold to drop holdings that are no longer in the file.
