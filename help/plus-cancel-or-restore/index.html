HelpStoxtox Plus

Cancel or restore Stoxtox Plus

Plus is billed by Apple. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings, restore from the Plus screen.

Plus is US$49.99 a year or US$5.99 a month in the US, and new subscribers get 3 months free. The App Store shows the price where you are.

Cancel

  1. Open You and tap Manage subscription.
  2. Or open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Stoxtox Plus and cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends. You keep Plus until then.

Restore on a new iPhone

  1. Sign in to the same Stoxtox account.
  2. Tap any Plus feature to open the Plus screen.
  3. Tap Restore purchases at the bottom.

"No Stoxtox Plus subscription on this Apple ID" means Plus was bought with a different Apple Account.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
