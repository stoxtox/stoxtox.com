What your net worth counts

Everything you own, minus what you owe, in your home currency.

Your net worth on Home adds up every account in Stoxtox. Debts like a mortgage, a loan or a credit card are taken away.

Leave something out

  1. On Home or Accounts, tap the filter button.
  2. Switch off anything you don't want counted, then close it.

Home then says Net worth, filtered. What you leave out is still in Accounts. Leaving out a home also leaves out its mortgage. Tap Show all to count everything again.

Your currency

Everything is converted into your home currency. To change it, open You, tap Home currency and pick one. Each account keeps its own currency too, with your home-currency amount beside it.
