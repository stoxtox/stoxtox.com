What Stoxtox does

Stoxtox adds up everything you own into one live net worth, in your own currency.

Stoxtox puts your brokers, banks, retirement accounts, crypto, property and gold in one place. Then it shows the total as one number, your net worth, and what moved it today.

It also explains what you own. Inside reads a company's reports in plain words. X-ray shows what your funds really hold. Performance shows how you did against the market.

What it doesn't do

  • It can't trade, move money or change anything at your bank or broker. Connections are read-only.
  • It doesn't give advice. Everything is for information only.

Free and Plus

Stoxtox is free to use with accounts you add by hand or import. Stoxtox Plus adds US connections, live prices, Performance, Inside, X-ray and more.
