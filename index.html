Markets and stocks

The markets you own, the moment they open.

The clock up top follows the markets you actually hold, open first: Mumbai in the morning, New York in the afternoon. Pull up for the day underneath: the mood, gold, oil and Bitcoin, every sector, and the biggest movers.

Every stock page knows the exchange’s own hours. During the session the line stops at now. Your position, cost and total return sit right under it.
