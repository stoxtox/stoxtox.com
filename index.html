Connect it, or type it in.

Connect your broker

Every currency

Dollars and rupees, added up properly.

Each account keeps its own currency. Your total converts at today’s exchange rates, so a demat account in Mumbai and a brokerage in Boston end up in one number, in the currency you think in.

Stock pages

A chart that knows what time it is.

During the trading day the line stops at now, on the exchange’s own clock, instead of stretching across the screen. Drag to see any moment. Change the range and the line reshapes instead of blinking.

Bitcoin and other coins are priced as coins, around the clock. Shares in London, Mumbai or Toronto show in their own currency.

Markets

The whole market, one pull away.

Your watchlist sits on top. Pull up for the day underneath: the market’s mood, gold, oil and Bitcoin, every sector at a glance, the biggest movers, earnings from companies you own, and what members of Congress have been trading.

Tap the eye. Hide amounts blurs your balances in one tap, for the train, the office or a shared screen. Prices stay visible.

  • It can look. It can’t touch.

    Stoxtox sees holdings and balances. It can’t trade, move money or change anything at your broker.

  • Your broker login stays with your broker

    You sign in on Plaid’s screen or your broker’s own. Your password never reaches us.

  • Locked with Face ID

    Turn on Face ID lock and Stoxtox asks every time it opens. It hides in the app switcher, too.

  • Yours to take, or delete

    Export your accounts and holdings as a spreadsheet whenever you like. Delete your account and it’s gone from our servers.
