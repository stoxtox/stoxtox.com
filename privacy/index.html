Stoxtox Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Stoxtox LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("Stoxtox", "we"), makes the Stoxtox app for iPhone and runs stoxtox.com. We decide what happens to the information described here. You can reach us at support@stoxtox.com.

Stoxtox shows your accounts, investments and net worth in one place. It is read-only: it can't move money, place trades or change anything at your bank or broker.

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

2. The short version

  • We keep what the app needs to show you your own money, and nothing for anyone else.

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, and we never sell your data.

  • You can use Stoxtox without an account. Your accounts and holdings then stay on your phone.

  • You can delete your account in the app at any time, and your data on our servers goes with it.

3. Your Stoxtox account

An account lets your data come back on a new phone and lets us send you notifications. You can sign in three ways:

  • Email and password. We keep your email address. Your password is stored only as a hash, so we never see it. We email you a code to confirm your address, and a link if you reset your password.

  • Sign in with Apple. We ask Apple only for your email address. If you choose Hide My Email, we get an Apple relay address instead of your real one.

  • Google. Google shares your email address, name and profile picture link with us. We keep them with your account but don't show or use your name or picture.

With every account we also keep a user ID, when you signed up and last signed in, and the IP address used to sign in.

5. Connecting US accounts with Plaid

In the US, you can connect a brokerage, retirement, bank, credit card or loan account through Plaid Inc. Connecting is optional and needs Stoxtox Plus. You can always add accounts by hand instead.

  • You sign in to your institution on Plaid's own screen. We never see or store your password.

  • From brokers, Plaid sends us each account's name, type, last 4 digits and balance, and its holdings: ticker, name, quantity, cost, price and value.

  • From banks, card issuers and lenders, Plaid sends us each account's name, type, last 4 digits and balance. We use Plaid's Transactions product to get those balances, but we never ask for your transactions.

  • Plaid gives our server an access token for your connection. It stays on our server, never goes to your phone, and is used only to refresh your own accounts.

  • On our server we keep the token, the institution's name, the latest copy of your connected accounts and holdings (refreshed about once a day), and any accounts you've removed from Stoxtox.

When you connect an account, you allow Stoxtox and Plaid to access and transfer this information from your institution on your behalf. Plaid handles your information under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect at any time in the app. That ends the connection at Plaid and deletes its token and saved copy from our server.

7. Market data and logos

Prices, charts, company facts, news, dividends and exchange rates come from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP). Your phone asks our server, and our server asks FMP. FMP sees the tickers and search words, but not your phone, your account or your portfolio. Our server keeps FMP's answers, including search results, for a short time (a few days at most) so other people asking the same thing get them faster. These aren't tied to anyone.

Company and coin logos are loaded by your phone straight from Logo.dev, with FMP's image server as a fallback. Those services see your IP address and which ticker or company website a logo is for. Images in notifications come from Logo.dev the same way.

If you open a news story or a company's website, it opens in a Safari window and that site's own privacy policy applies.

8. Stoxtox Plus

Stoxtox Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details.

When you buy or renew, Apple sends us a signed record of the purchase, tagged with your Stoxtox user ID so we can match it to you. We keep the plan, Apple's transaction ID, whether it's active, when it renews or ends, whether it will renew, and whether you're in a free trial. We use this only to turn Plus on and off and to remind you two days before a free trial ends.

9. Notifications and email

If you allow notifications, the app gives our server your phone's push token. We send notifications through Apple Push Notification service, so their text, which can mention your holdings and how much they moved, passes through Apple. You can turn each kind off in You → Notifications, or all of them in iOS Settings.

Sign-up codes and password reset emails are sent through Resend. We don't send marketing email.

10. Crash reports and logs

When the app crashes, hangs or uses too much battery or storage, iOS writes a report and hands it to the app the next time it opens. The app sends it to us with the app version, iOS version and iPhone model, and your user ID if you're signed in. Reports hold the app's code paths, not your portfolio. We use them only to fix bugs.

Like any website or server, ours also log requests, including IP address and time, for a short period to keep the service running and stop abuse.

12. Security

  • Every connection uses HTTPS. Our database is encrypted at rest.

  • The database itself makes sure each person can read only their own data.

  • Plaid tokens and our service keys stay on our server and are never sent to the app.

  • On your iPhone, your data is kept in the app's private storage. Each person who signs in on a phone has their own copy, and signing out hides yours.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

13. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete something in the app, we keep a small marker for it so your other phones remove it too.

Deleting your account. Go to You → Delete account. The app then:

  • if you used Sign in with Apple, asks Apple to confirm and revokes Stoxtox's access to your Apple ID;

  • ends each Plaid connection at Plaid;

  • deletes your account and everything we keep about you in sections 3 to 9 from our servers straight away, and removes your data from that phone.

Crash reports are kept after that, but with your user ID removed, so they no longer point to you. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are gone within 90 days. Deleting your account doesn't cancel Stoxtox Plus; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings so Apple stops billing you. Apple, Google and Plaid keep their own records under their own policies.
