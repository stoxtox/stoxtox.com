Stoxtox Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Stoxtox LLC ("Stoxtox", "we") makes the Stoxtox app for iPhone, which shows your net worth and investments in one place. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to stoxtox.com.

Stoxtox is read-only. It cannot move money, place trades or make changes at your bank or broker, and it does not give investment advice.

Effective date: 20 October 2026.

3. How we use it

Your information is used only to run Stoxtox for you.

  • Show your accounts, holdings, net worth and how they change over time.

  • Value your holdings at current market prices.

  • Keep your data in sync across your devices when you sign in.

  • Keep your account secure and prevent fraud and abuse.

  • Answer your questions and send important notices about your account.

  • Meet legal obligations.

We never sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use your financial data to market other products to you.

4. Connecting accounts with Plaid

In the US, you can connect a brokerage or retirement account through Plaid Inc. Connecting is optional; you can always add accounts by hand instead.

  • You sign in to your broker on Plaid's own screen. Stoxtox never sees or stores your password.

  • Plaid sends us your account details, balances and holdings. It does not send us transactions.

  • Plaid gives our server a private key for your connection. That key stays on our server, is never sent to your phone, and is used only to refresh your own holdings.

  • Access is read-only. Neither Stoxtox nor Plaid can move money or place trades through this connection.

When you connect an account, you allow Stoxtox and Plaid to access and transfer this information from your broker on your behalf. Plaid handles your information under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect at any time in the app. That ends the connection at Plaid and deletes its accounts and key from Stoxtox.

6. How we protect and keep it

Your data is stored in the United States and encrypted both in transit and at rest.

  • Every connection uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Our database is encrypted at rest with AES-256.

  • Each person can reach only their own data; this is enforced by the database itself.

  • On your iPhone, data stays in the app's private storage, protected by iOS while your phone is locked. Signing out hides it, and other people who sign in on the same phone can't see it.

  • Plaid connection keys are held only by our server and never shown in the app.

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete it from our servers right away. Copies in backups are removed within 90 days. Some records may be kept longer if the law requires it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.
