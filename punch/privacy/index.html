Who we are

Punch is made by Stoxtox LLC. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the Punch app for iPhone.

What the app keeps, and where

Punch saves the things you enter so it can do its job:

  • Your shifts: when you punched in and out, your meal breaks, and the sales you logged.
  • Your pay settings: hourly rate, commission, overtime, whether meals are paid, and the day your work week starts.
  • Small app preferences, like the buddy you picked and which reminders are on.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. Punch has no account and no sign-in, and it never sends this information to Stoxtox LLC or to anyone else.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or measure how you use the app.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't include third-party code that collects data.

Reminders and the lock screen

If you turn on reminders, your iPhone schedules them itself. Nothing is sent to a server to make them work.

The lock screen and Dynamic Island show how long you've been working. They never show what you've earned.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes Punch's data in that backup, the same as for other apps. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy. Stoxtox LLC has no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any shift inside the app, from Week, then All shifts. Deleting the Punch app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Punch is meant for people who work. It isn't aimed at children under 13, and since it collects nothing, it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
