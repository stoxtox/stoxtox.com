Common questions

How do I punch in or out?

Press and hold the big button on the Today screen to punch in. To punch out, press and hold "Hold to punch out". Holding, instead of tapping, means a stray tap can't clock you in or out.

I forgot to punch out. Can I fix the shift?

Yes. Go to Week, then All shifts, open the shift and tap "Fix this shift". You can move the punch in and punch out times, change the meal, and add or remove sales.

If you forgot to punch in entirely, use "Add a shift".

How does overtime work?

Turn on Overtime in the Pay tab and pick 1.5x or 2x. Paid hours past 40 in your work week are paid at that rate. You can also choose the day your work week starts.

Are the pay amounts exact?

They're estimates before taxes, worked out from the rate, commission and overtime you set. Your employer's payroll is the final word.

Why doesn't the lock screen show my pay?

On purpose. Anyone near your phone can see the lock screen, so it only shows time.

Punch didn't remind me to punch out.

Check that reminders are on in Pay, under "Nudge me", and that notifications are allowed for Punch in the Settings app.

For your first few shifts, Punch only reminds you after 15 hours on the clock. Once it has seen 5 shifts, it reminds you 2 hours after your longest recent shift.

Is my data backed up or synced?

Punch keeps everything on your iPhone. It's included in your normal iPhone backup, but it doesn't sync between devices.

How do I delete my data?

Delete shifts one at a time from All shifts, or delete the app to remove everything.
