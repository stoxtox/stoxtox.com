4Stoxtox Plus

Stoxtox is free to use. Stoxtox Plus is an optional subscription that adds features such as connecting US accounts, performance against the market, every chapter of Inside a company, X-ray of your funds, dividend forecasts, unlimited and smarter alerts, and monthly cards. The app shows what Plus includes and its current prices before you subscribe. You need a Stoxtox account to get Plus.

  • Billing. Plus is sold through Apple's App Store and charged to your Apple Account. It costs US$5.99 a month or US$49.99 a year in the US; the App Store shows the price in your country. Apple's terms apply to the purchase.

  • Free trial. Both plans start with a 3-month free trial for new subscribers. You are charged when it ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. If notifications are on, we remind you two days before it ends. The App Store allows one free trial per Apple Account.

  • Renewal. Plus renews automatically at the then-current price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

  • Cancelling. Cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your Stoxtox account doesn't cancel Plus, so cancel it in Settings too.

  • When Plus ends. Connected accounts are paused and held for 30 days. If Plus doesn't resume, we end the connections and your accounts stay in Stoxtox as saved copies you can update by hand. Nothing you added is deleted.

  • Moving Plus. A subscription belongs to one Apple Account and works on one Stoxtox account at a time. You can move it to another Stoxtox account in the app; the account it leaves goes back to free.

  • Refunds. Apple handles refunds under its own policies; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

  • Changes. We may change what Plus includes or its price. A price increase applies only from your next renewal, and Apple asks for your consent where its rules require it.

They may be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and values shown in Stoxtox may differ from your broker's statements. Always check your institution's records before relying on a number. Market data is provided for your personal use only and may not be copied, resold or redistributed.

Some of what Stoxtox shows is worked out by us and is approximate. Inside a company simplifies and rounds a company's reports, for example into amounts per $100 of sales. X-ray estimates what your funds own from the holdings they report, which can be out of date. Dividend forecasts, and history from before you joined, are estimates and marked that way.
